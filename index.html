Questions doctors ask

What exactly is CoverPrime?

A medico-legal support membership. Members can reach an advisor 24x7 for guidance on notices, complaints, disputes, compliance requirements and defamatory online content, plus documentation help and coordination when something happens. Benefits vary by plan and are subject to the Membership Wording.

Is this insurance?

No. CoverPrime is not insurance. It doesn't pay claims, compensation or government fees. It gives you expert guidance and support, and it can be used alongside any indemnity insurance you might already hold. Advocate fees, where a matter needs one, are as per the plan you choose.

Are you a law firm?

No. CoverPrime is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or representation. Our advisors provide guidance, documentation support and coordination. Where a matter needs a lawyer, independent advocates act for you. CoverPrime helps you connect with them and coordinates the process.

What happens after I fill the form?

A CoverPrime representative will contact you on the mobile number you share, to understand your practice and what you need. They will explain the membership options. There's no obligation to join. We'll use your details only as described in our Privacy Policy.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on whether you're an individual doctor, a clinic or a hospital, and is shared after a short consultation. Our representative will share exact pricing, including GST and what's included, before you decide. There's no obligation. Membership terms, renewal and cancellation are set out in the Terms of Membership.
