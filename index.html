Questions doctors ask

What exactly is CoverPrime?

A medico-legal support membership. Members get experienced advisors 24x7 for notices, complaints, disputes, compliance and reputation issues, plus documentation help and coordination when something happens.

Is this insurance?

No. CoverPrime doesn't pay claims or compensation. It gives you expert guidance and support. If you have indemnity insurance, CoverPrime works alongside it.

Are you lawyers?

CoverPrime is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation. Where a matter needs a lawyer, independent advocates handle it, and CoverPrime coordinates with them for you.

What happens after I fill the form?

Our expert will connect with you to understand your practice and what you need, then recommend a membership. There's no obligation to join.

How much does it cost?

It depends on whether you're an individual doctor, a clinic or a hospital. Our expert will share current pricing after understanding your practice.
